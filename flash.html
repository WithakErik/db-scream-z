This page needs WebUSB [Web Universal Serial Bus], which Chrome and Edge have and Firefox and Safari do not. Open it in Chrome or Edge on a computer.

1. Put the pedal in DFU [Device Firmware Upgrade] mode

  1. Connect the Seed's USB [Universal Serial Bus] port to this computer with a data cable, and power the pedal.
  2. Make sure the pedal is bypassed: both LEDs [light-emitting diodes] off.
  3. Press both stomps together and hold about 2 seconds. The pedal goes quiet and waits for the computer.

If that does not work: hold the BOOT button on the Seed, tap RESET, release BOOT.

Your saved voices and settings are kept. Flashing only replaces the firmware.

The pedal is plugged in but running normally. Enter DFU mode with the steps above, then press Connect again and pick "DFU in FS Mode".

On Linux, the browser needs permission to open the pedal. Add this rule once, then unplug the pedal and plug it back in:

echo 'SUBSYSTEM=="usb", ATTRS{idVendor}=="0483", ATTRS{idProduct}=="df11", TAG+="uaccess"' | sudo tee /etc/udev/rules.d/70-stm32-dfu.rules
sudo udevadm control --reload-rules
sudo udevadm trigger

On Windows, the DFU device may need the WinUSB driver. Electro-Smith's Zadig guide walks through it.

Nothing is broken. The bootloader lives in read-only memory and cannot be overwritten. Hold BOOT on the Seed, tap RESET, release BOOT, then press Connect and Flash again.
